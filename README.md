# Accountix ERP

Multi-company ERP: accounting, financial and executive reports, inventory with FIFO/average costing,
sales and purchase documents, invoice tracking, HR, payroll and provident fund, fixed assets,
FBR digital invoicing, and a super admin console.

- **The app:** [`dotnet/`](dotnet/README.md). .NET 10 (ASP.NET Core MVC, EF Core, PostgreSQL / Neon).
- **Status, speed and load results:** [`dotnet/docs/PROGRESS.md`](dotnet/docs/PROGRESS.md).
- **Deploy and switch-over:** [`dotnet/docs/DEPLOY.md`](dotnet/docs/DEPLOY.md), [`dotnet/docs/CUTOVER.md`](dotnet/docs/CUTOVER.md).
- **Business and design documents:** [`docs/`](docs/). Accounting policy, the documents UI standard, the design system, the invoice calculation rules.
- **Working rules:** [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

The app was Python/Flask until 29 Oct 2026; [`docs/HISTORY_PYTHON_ERA.md`](docs/HISTORY_PYTHON_ERA.md) keeps that history.
